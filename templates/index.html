How CampusSkill Works

Four simple steps to start learning or teaching a skill on campus.

👤
Step 01

Create Your Profile

Sign up with your student email and build a profile listing your skills and interests.

🔍
Step 02

Discover Skills

Search and filter by skill category or proficiency level to find the right peer teacher.

📩
Step 03

Send a Request

Send a personalized learning request with your preferred schedule to the skill provider.

⭐
Step 04

Learn & Rate

Attend your session, grow your skills, and leave a rating to help the community.
